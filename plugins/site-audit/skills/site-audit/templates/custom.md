# My site-audit rules

Copy this file to ~/.config/site-audit/custom.md (your rules), or to site-audit.custom.md in your
working folder (rules shared by everyone who audits from that folder). Plugin updates never touch
either copy. Your rules take priority over the skill's own files; a rule in the working folder
wins over one in ~/.config. Only the safety rules cannot be changed: the audit never places an
order, enters payment details or a password, or submits a form.

Write plain sentences under the headings you need and delete the rest. Empty headings are ignored.
